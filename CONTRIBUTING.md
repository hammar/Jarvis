# Contribution and support policy

Jarvis is an owner-directed personal experiment in agentic development,
not a community-maintained project.

Unsolicited external pull requests are not accepted and may be closed
without review. Please do not invest time in a contribution expecting it
to be merged. Owner-directed agent work is not an invitation for external
contributions.

Non-sensitive bug reports may be filed as GitHub issues. Responses,
investigation, fixes and feature requests are not guaranteed. Do not include
credentials, private conversations, household information, real device
identifiers or unredacted logs, screenshots or traces.

For suspected vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of
posting details publicly.

Public visibility does not grant a general reuse license. The
[rights notice](RIGHTS.md) describes the licensing position; this policy
does not grant additional permissions.
